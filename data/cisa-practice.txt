1) When developing a risk-based IS audit plan, what should the IS auditor do FIRST?
A. Select the controls to be tested
B. Obtain an understanding of the organization's business processes and objectives
C. Determine the sample sizes for substantive testing
D. Schedule audit staff for each engagement
Answer: B
Explanation: A risk-based plan starts from the business. Risks, and the controls that address them, cannot be identified until the auditor understands the processes and objectives they support.

2) An IS auditor wants to estimate the total monetary value of errors in a population of invoices. Which sampling method is MOST appropriate?
A. Attribute sampling
B. Discovery sampling
C. Variable sampling
D. Stop-or-go sampling
Answer: C
Explanation: Variable sampling estimates a monetary amount or other quantity. Attribute sampling estimates the rate of occurrence of a characteristic, and discovery sampling looks for at least one occurrence.

3) Which of the following audit evidence is MOST reliable?
A. A system report provided by the auditee
B. A confirmation letter received directly from an independent third party
C. A verbal statement from the IT manager
D. Screenshots supplied by the system administrator
Answer: B
Explanation: Evidence from an independent source, received directly by the auditor, is more reliable than evidence produced or passed on by the auditee.

4) An IS auditor helped design the controls for a new payroll system and is now asked to audit it. What is the BEST course of action?
A. Perform the audit, because knowledge of the design will make the audit more efficient
B. Disclose the potential impairment of independence to audit management before accepting the assignment
C. Ask the system developer to review the audit findings
D. Reduce the audit scope to controls the auditor did not design
Answer: B
Explanation: Auditing one's own work impairs objectivity. The impairment must be disclosed so that audit management can decide how to handle the assignment.

5) Which continuous audit technique creates dummy entities in production files and processes test transactions against them alongside live data?
A. Snapshots
B. Integrated test facility (ITF)
C. System control audit review file (SCARF)
D. Audit hooks
Answer: B
Explanation: An ITF sets up fictitious entities in the live environment and compares the processing results against expected results.

6) During fieldwork, an IS auditor reports a significant control weakness, and the auditee fixes it before the audit report is issued. What should the auditor do?
A. Remove the finding from the report, because it has been resolved
B. Include the finding in the report and note that corrective action was taken
C. Report the finding verbally to management only
D. Delay the report until the fix has been in place for six months
Answer: B
Explanation: The weakness existed during the audit period, so it belongs in the report. Noting the corrective action gives a complete and fair picture.

7) What is the PRIMARY benefit of a control self-assessment (CSA) program?
A. It replaces the need for internal audit
B. It increases business line ownership of and accountability for controls
C. It reduces the cost of external audits
D. It eliminates the need for management to monitor controls
Answer: B
Explanation: CSA involves process owners in evaluating their own controls, which strengthens their ownership. It complements internal audit rather than replacing it.

8) The authority, scope and responsibilities of the IS audit function should be documented in the:
A. audit engagement letter
B. audit charter approved by the board or audit committee
C. IT strategic plan
D. annual audit plan
Answer: B
Explanation: The audit charter establishes the audit function's authority and accountability. Engagement letters cover individual engagements.

9) What is the PRIMARY advantage of statistical sampling over nonstatistical (judgmental) sampling?
A. It always requires a smaller sample
B. It allows the auditor to quantify sampling risk objectively
C. It removes the need for auditor judgment
D. It guarantees that all errors will be found
Answer: B
Explanation: Statistical sampling lets the auditor measure the probability that the sample is not representative (sampling risk). Judgment is still needed, and it guarantees nothing.

10) Which body is PRIMARILY responsible for advising the board on the alignment of IT strategy with business strategy?
A. IT steering committee
B. IT strategy committee
C. Project management office
D. Change advisory board
Answer: B
Explanation: The IT strategy committee operates at board level and advises on strategic alignment. The steering committee operates at executive level and oversees projects and priorities.

11) Who is ultimately responsible for determining the classification of data?
A. The data custodian
B. The security administrator
C. The data owner
D. The database administrator
Answer: C
Explanation: Data owners are business managers accountable for the data. They decide its classification and who may access it. Custodians implement their decisions.

12) Which combination of duties poses the GREATEST risk?
A. An application programmer with write access to production program libraries
B. A network administrator who also staffs the help desk
C. A security administrator who reviews access logs
D. A quality assurance analyst who executes test scripts
Answer: A
Explanation: A programmer who can change production code can introduce unauthorized changes without passing through change control, which defeats segregation of duties.

13) In a small organization, one person performs both system administration and security administration. What is the BEST compensating control?
A. Requiring the person to take annual leave
B. Independent management review of that person's activity logs
C. Increasing the password length for that person's account
D. Documenting the job description
Answer: B
Explanation: When duties cannot be separated, an independent review of the person's activity detects misuse of the combined privileges.

14) Who is responsible for accepting residual risk on behalf of the organization?
A. The IS auditor
B. The chief information security officer
C. Senior management
D. The system administrator
Answer: C
Explanation: Risk acceptance is a business decision made by senior management within the risk appetite set by the board. Auditors and security staff advise but do not accept risk.

15) What is the PRIMARY purpose of an IT balanced scorecard?
A. To track the IT department's budget variances
B. To measure IT performance against business-aligned objectives beyond financial measures
C. To rank IT staff for performance reviews
D. To replace service level agreements
Answer: B
Explanation: A balanced scorecard evaluates IT from several perspectives (business contribution, user orientation, operational excellence and future orientation) to show alignment with business goals.

16) An organization outsources its data center operations. Accountability for protecting customer data remains with:
A. the service provider
B. the organization that outsourced the operations
C. the external auditor
D. the regulator
Answer: B
Explanation: Tasks can be outsourced, but accountability cannot. The organization must oversee the provider through the contract, monitoring and audit rights.

17) When reviewing an information security policy, what is it MOST important for the IS auditor to confirm?
A. It is based on a recognized framework
B. It has been approved by senior management
C. It is longer than the previous version
D. It lists technical configuration settings
Answer: B
Explanation: Senior management approval gives the policy its authority and shows management commitment. Technical settings belong in standards and procedures.

18) What is the PRIMARY purpose of a right-to-audit clause in an outsourcing contract?
A. To reduce the cost of the service
B. To allow the organization to verify the effectiveness of the provider's controls
C. To transfer liability for breaches to the provider
D. To guarantee service availability
Answer: B
Explanation: A right-to-audit clause lets the organization (or its auditors) obtain assurance over the controls the provider operates on its behalf.

19) What is the PRIMARY objective of user acceptance testing (UAT)?
A. To verify that the code meets programming standards
B. To confirm that the system meets documented business requirements
C. To test system performance under peak load
D. To ensure that individual modules interface correctly
Answer: B
Explanation: UAT is performed by users to confirm that the system does what the business needs before it is accepted into production.

20) When should the business case for a project be reviewed?
A. Only at project initiation
B. Only after implementation
C. At key decision points throughout the project life cycle
D. Only when the budget is exceeded
Answer: C
Explanation: Revisiting the business case at each key decision point confirms that the project is still justified and allows it to be stopped or redirected early.

21) What is the PRIMARY objective of a post-implementation review?
A. To identify programming errors
B. To determine whether the project's objectives and expected benefits were achieved
C. To evaluate the performance of the project team
D. To close the project budget
Answer: B
Explanation: A post-implementation review assesses whether the system delivered what the business case promised and captures lessons learned.

22) What is the BEST control over emergency changes to production systems?
A. Prohibiting emergency changes entirely
B. Documenting the change and obtaining retroactive approval through the normal change process
C. Allowing only senior developers to make emergency changes
D. Recording the change in the help desk system only
Answer: B
Explanation: Emergency changes are sometimes necessary, but they must still be documented, reviewed and approved after the fact so that nothing escapes change control.

23) What is the PRIMARY purpose of parallel testing when implementing a new system?
A. To test the system's performance under stress
B. To compare the results of the new system with those of the old system
C. To test each module independently
D. To train users on the new system
Answer: B
Explanation: In parallel testing, both systems process the same data so that differences in results can be identified before the old system is retired.

24) Which is the MOST important control when migrating data to a new system?
A. Encrypting the data during transfer
B. Reconciling record counts and control totals between the old and new systems
C. Deleting the old data immediately after migration
D. Having developers verify the migration
Answer: B
Explanation: Reconciling record counts and control totals shows that data was transferred completely and accurately.

25) Which metric defines the maximum amount of data an organization can afford to lose, measured in time?
A. Recovery time objective (RTO)
B. Recovery point objective (RPO)
C. Maximum tolerable downtime (MTD)
D. Service delivery objective (SDO)
Answer: B
Explanation: RPO is the point in time to which data must be restored, so it drives backup frequency. RTO is how quickly service must be restored.

26) A critical system has a recovery time objective of a few minutes. Which recovery strategy is MOST appropriate?
A. Cold site
B. Warm site
C. Hot site with data mirroring
D. Reciprocal agreement
Answer: C
Explanation: A very short RTO requires a fully equipped site with current data. Cold and warm sites, and reciprocal agreements, take hours to days to activate.

27) What is the FIRST step in developing a business continuity plan?
A. Selecting a recovery site
B. Performing a business impact analysis (BIA)
C. Testing the plan
D. Training staff
Answer: B
Explanation: The BIA identifies critical processes, their impact and their recovery objectives. These results drive every later decision in the plan.

28) Which business continuity test is the MOST thorough without disrupting normal operations?
A. Checklist review
B. Tabletop walk-through
C. Parallel test
D. Full interruption test
Answer: C
Explanation: A parallel test brings up the recovery site and processes real data while production continues. A full interruption test is more thorough but disrupts operations.

29) What is the PRIMARY objective of problem management?
A. To restore service as quickly as possible
B. To identify the root cause of incidents and prevent recurrence
C. To log all user calls to the help desk
D. To approve changes to production
Answer: B
Explanation: Incident management restores service. Problem management finds and removes underlying causes so that incidents do not recur.

30) What is the MOST important consideration when selecting an offsite backup storage location?
A. It is close enough for staff to reach quickly
B. It is not subject to the same threats as the primary site
C. It is owned by the organization
D. It has the lowest storage cost
Answer: B
Explanation: A storage site that shares the primary site's risks, such as the same flood zone or power grid, may be lost in the same disaster.

31) Which RAID level provides disk mirroring?
A. RAID 0
B. RAID 1
C. RAID 5
D. RAID 6
Answer: B
Explanation: RAID 1 writes identical data to two disks. RAID 0 stripes with no redundancy, and RAID 5 and 6 use striping with parity.

32) Which BEST allows a database to be restored to a consistent state after a system failure?
A. Daily full backups only
B. Transaction logging with before-images and after-images
C. Database encryption
D. Referential integrity constraints
Answer: B
Explanation: Before-images and after-images let incomplete transactions be rolled back and committed transactions be rolled forward after a failure.

33) Which audit procedure is MOST effective for detecting unauthorized changes to production programs?
A. Reviewing change request forms
B. Comparing production program versions against the last authorized versions
C. Interviewing developers
D. Reviewing the change management policy
Answer: B
Explanation: Code comparison finds changes that bypassed the change process, which a review of change forms would never show.

34) How can an organization BEST verify that an IT service provider meets its service level agreement?
A. Relying on the provider's assurances
B. Regularly reviewing performance reports against the SLA metrics
C. Renegotiating the contract annually
D. Increasing the penalties in the contract
Answer: B
Explanation: SLAs are only useful if performance is measured and reviewed against the agreed metrics, with deviations followed up.

35) An organization has confirmed a malware outbreak on several workstations. According to the incident response lifecycle, what should it do NEXT?
A. Eradicate the malware
B. Contain the incident
C. Conduct a lessons-learned review
D. Restore systems from backup
Answer: B
Explanation: After an incident is identified, containment limits the damage and stops the spread. Eradication, recovery and lessons learned follow.

36) What is the GREATEST risk of spreadsheets used for financial reporting (end-user computing)?
A. They require expensive licenses
B. Errors may go undetected because of a lack of change control and testing
C. They are slower than enterprise applications
D. Users need training to use them
Answer: B
Explanation: End-user computing tools often bypass formal development, testing and change controls, so errors in formulas or data can reach financial reports unnoticed.

37) Which change management weakness should concern an IS auditor MOST?
A. Changes are approved by a change advisory board
B. Developers can move their own changes into production without independent review
C. Changes are scheduled outside business hours
D. Change requests are recorded in a ticketing system
Answer: B
Explanation: Without segregation between development and migration to production, unauthorized or untested changes can reach production.

38) Access rights should be granted according to which principle?
A. Seniority
B. Least privilege and need to know
C. Convenience for users
D. Department membership only
Answer: B
Explanation: Users should receive only the access needed for their job duties. This limits the damage from errors, misuse and compromised accounts.

39) What is the MOST effective control to ensure that terminated employees lose access promptly?
A. An annual review of user accounts
B. Prompt notification from HR to the security administrator, triggering immediate deactivation
C. Password expiry every 90 days
D. Requiring managers to recover ID badges
Answer: B
Explanation: A defined process linking HR terminations to immediate access removal closes the window of exposure. Periodic reviews find stale accounts only after the fact.

40) A digital signature provides all of the following EXCEPT:
A. integrity
B. authentication of the sender
C. non-repudiation
D. confidentiality
Answer: D
Explanation: A digital signature proves who sent a message and that it was not altered. It does not hide the content. Confidentiality requires encryption.

41) To create a digital signature, the sender encrypts the message hash with the:
A. sender's public key
B. sender's private key
C. recipient's public key
D. recipient's private key
Answer: B
Explanation: Only the sender holds their private key, so anyone can verify the signature with the sender's public key.

42) To send a message that only the intended recipient can read using public key cryptography, the sender encrypts it with the:
A. sender's private key
B. sender's public key
C. recipient's public key
D. recipient's private key
Answer: C
Explanation: Anything encrypted with the recipient's public key can be decrypted only with the recipient's private key.

43) Which type of firewall can inspect the content of traffic at the application layer?
A. Packet-filtering router
B. Stateful inspection firewall
C. Application-level gateway (proxy firewall)
D. Circuit-level gateway
Answer: C
Explanation: An application-level gateway understands application protocols and can filter based on content. Packet filters and stateful firewalls work mainly with header and session information.

44) When comparing the accuracy of biometric systems, which measure is the BEST overall indicator?
A. False acceptance rate (FAR)
B. False rejection rate (FRR)
C. Crossover error rate (CER)
D. Enrollment time
Answer: C
Explanation: The CER is the point where FAR equals FRR. The lower the CER, the more accurate the system overall.

45) What is the MOST effective control against social engineering attacks?
A. Stronger firewalls
B. Security awareness training for staff
C. Intrusion detection systems
D. Encryption of stored data
Answer: B
Explanation: Social engineering targets people rather than technology, so educating staff to recognize and report such attempts is the most effective defense.

46) Which measure MOST strengthens user authentication?
A. Longer password expiry periods
B. Multifactor authentication
C. Displaying the last login time
D. Allowing password reuse
Answer: B
Explanation: MFA requires two or more factors (something you know, have or are), so a stolen password alone is not enough to gain access.

47) Which method is MOST effective for ensuring that data cannot be recovered from decommissioned hard drives?
A. Deleting all files
B. Reformatting the drives
C. Physically destroying the drives
D. Removing the partition table
Answer: C
Explanation: Deleting files, reformatting and removing partitions leave data recoverable. Physical destruction makes recovery infeasible.

48) Which configuration provides the STRONGEST security for a corporate wireless network?
A. WEP with a shared key
B. WPA2-Personal with a pre-shared key
C. WPA3-Enterprise with 802.1X authentication
D. A hidden SSID with MAC address filtering
Answer: C
Explanation: Enterprise mode authenticates each user individually through a server, and WPA3 provides stronger encryption. WEP is broken, and hidden SSIDs and MAC filtering are easily bypassed.

49) Which control can both detect and automatically block malicious network traffic?
A. Intrusion detection system (IDS)
B. Intrusion prevention system (IPS)
C. Security information and event management (SIEM)
D. Network traffic analyzer
Answer: B
Explanation: An IPS sits inline and can drop malicious traffic. An IDS only detects and alerts, and a SIEM correlates and reports events.

50) What is MOST important for audit logs to serve as reliable evidence?
A. They are stored in plain text for easy reading
B. They are protected from unauthorized modification or deletion
C. They are kept on the same server as the application
D. They are reviewed only after an incident
Answer: B
Explanation: If logs can be altered by the people whose activity they record, they cannot be relied on. Write-once storage or restricted access protects their integrity.
